also produces: 28/121 = 23% of questions are single-date, so four losses expect 0.93 there.

## What this artifact does not carry

`AblationReport.questions` holds one roster, so `promptDigest` is one per question and the two sides'
prompts cannot be compared from this file. `C2`/`C3` (non-`MR` prompts byte-identical, delivered
multiset unchanged) are design preconditions carried by `turn-clock.test.ts` and
`test_session_clock_wiring.py`, as the pre-registration says — not by this artifact.
