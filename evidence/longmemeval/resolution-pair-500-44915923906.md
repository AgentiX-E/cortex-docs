`G5`'s absolute form was written for the 500-question `reference`-against-`cortex` reading (7,448), and
this dispatch has `cortex` on both sides, so the comparable statement is the ratio: the bill did not fall.
That is the clause's substance — *"a mechanism whose other half is cost loses its reason when the bill
rises"* — and it fails it.

## What the reading settles

1. **The mechanism reallocates.** Characters leave (2.81%), turns stay or rise (+3.69% total, 235 questions
   with more turns against 6 with fewer), and nothing is removed from the selector. That is the signature
   `C8` predicted and it is the opposite of `C7`'s density prediction.
2. **The saving is smaller than the floor.** 2.81% against a 3% floor is a fail, and it closes the claim
   as registered. The next reader should not re-open it by moving the floor to 2.5%.
3. **Characters are not tokens.** The bill rose 0.18% while the characters fell 2.81%. This is the second
   independent measurement of the same inversion (`44905275457`: chars −5.2%, tokens +0.75%), and the two
   together are the law: `record` keeps the digit-bearing sentences and `excerpt` keeps the densest window,
   so what leaves is prose and what stays tokenises worse per character.
4. **The accuracy effect is real, replicates, and is `MR`-specific.** +2.40 points overall at p = 0.0884 is
   not significance; `MR` at +7.85 with 15 up / 6 down in an independent run, on top of +6.20 in the first,
   is a consistent finding about one capability.
